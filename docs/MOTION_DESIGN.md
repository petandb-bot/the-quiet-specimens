# Reborn motion design

The simulation advances in fixed steps of 1/60 second. The renderer caps catch-up after a slow frame or a return from another tab. The sound scheduler uses its own Web Audio clock.

A specimen chooses a local way point, accelerates toward it, and decelerates with a damped velocity as it approaches. Yaw follows velocity along the shorter angle. Way points and movement stay within the deck. Scenic objects supply soft perimeter fields to guide paths. Physically accurate contacts and individual-to-individual behavior are later studies.

Gestures pass through hesitation, action, recovery, and rest. The travel cadence drives the walking rig, while the gesture envelope controls folding and hopping. Vessel's envelope and interior obey separate signals. Relay propagates a phase delay through a chain with expanding, contracting, and winding links. Tap reactions run with a cooldown. Action mode is a manual trigger on the inspection copy of a specimen.

The shared audio scheduler follows one specimen with a four-voice ceiling. The current phase affects event likelihood and note length through the existing sound controls. The selected specimen can be auditioned without starting a permanent stream.
