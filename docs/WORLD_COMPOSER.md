# World Composer

Open **Make**, choose a form in **Objects**, and click or tap the ground to place it. Size and finish controls apply to the next placements. **Randomize** changes the brush's seed and look. **Done** or Escape ends placement. **Edit last** opens the most recently placed object.

Click, tap, or drag an object to select it. Drag it over the terrain to move it. The contextual controls offer size, finish, a rotation step, duplicate, **Mutate**, and delete. Mutate changes the seed and preserves the position, size and chosen finish. **Advanced** includes coordinates, fine rotation, seed and height. Click empty ground to deselect.

Hold Space and drag to orbit or use right drag to pan. Scroll to zoom. On touch, use two fingers to pan and pinch to zoom. Use **Camera** for a one-finger camera drag. **Plan** gives you a top-down work view. **Hide tools** folds the dock while keeping the canvas ready. **?** opens shortcuts and quick start.

**Land** adjusts terrain. **Atmosphere** opens surface, lighting, sky, fog and local photo controls. Close either panel with its Close button, Escape or a tap on the canvas. **Generate** chooses a world style and rerolls unlocked layers. [Generator controls](WORLD_GENERATOR.md)

**Life** manages residents and Places. Link a Place to a structure or draw an independent region with a click. **Neutral** takes priority over the structure's automatic interest. Set individual starts, home areas and preferred areas in **Residents**. A placement on blocked ground can use a nearby safe point. Inspect and Follow return to Observe.

The activity selector offers **Paused**, **Slow** at 30%, and **Live** at normal speed. Make starts at Slow, or Paused when the system prefers reduced motion. Observe resumes the prior observation camera and activity setting. Sound works in both. **Focus** hides the observation controls.

Undo and Redo cover placements, complete drags, transforms, generation and locks. Use Ctrl/Cmd+Z or Ctrl/Cmd+Shift+Z. A world can hold 40 objects, 24 Places and nine residents.

## Local working draft

One scene and its accepted photo are saved automatically in this browser profile. Reload restores the layout, environment and resident settings. Camera, live motion and Undo history start fresh. Clearing site storage removes the draft. A newer draft saved in another tab is protected from being overwritten. Reload to open it.
