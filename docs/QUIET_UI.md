# Quiet Observation UI

**Observe** and **Make** are available at the top of the window. The opening Observe view keeps the title as a semantic heading and gives the rendered world the full frame. The small home mark carries a descriptive accessible name.

Observe offers **Residents**, **Camera**, and **Focus** in the header. Focus hides the interface while the simulation and sound continue. Residents opens the browser and leads to Discover, Inspect, Action, and Soft Follow. Inspect and Action show the selected specimen name as a small label.

**Sound** is available in both workspaces. **More** offers **Sound mix** and **Legacy**. It closes when a panel opens, the user clicks outside, or they press Escape.
