# Still & Alive — Audio

**Sound** starts the Web Audio session on a user gesture. The master output ramps in and the first scheduled note bypasses the probability gate. The interface distinguishes a blocked context, tab visibility, zero volume and an empty world. **Audio Reset** fades out the active graph and leaves the sound button ready to start a fresh context.

**World** gives each resident a seed and motif, an independent interval and a musical role derived from its sonotype. Cohorts rotate between two and four participants. The scheduler uses the AudioContext clock, with separate limits for simultaneous voices, sustained voices, and new entries. Longer phrase gaps leave room for other residents. Choosing a resident changes Solo and Audition without resetting World. **Solo** plays one resident. **Audition** lasts about ten audio-clock seconds and returns to the previous mix and sound state.

Shelter and Quiet reduce density. Curiosity can brighten a note and Gathering can make a response more likely. Overlapping places take their strongest effect per role. An encounter or imitation can affect a subsequent note on a separate cooldown; notes do not produce further simulation events. World position contributes a limited stereo offset. The listening mix is independent of the observation camera.

The graph uses a high-pass filter, peak guard, and master fade. Browser checks exercised rendered signals at 44.1 and 48 kHz. [Verification](VERIFICATION.md) · [Short World Audio capture](audio/still-and-alive.wav)
