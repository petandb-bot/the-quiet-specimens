# Still & Alive — Observation

The opening **Fixed** view is a stationary frame. **Residents** shows live activity and profile thumbnails rendered from each specimen's Three.js form. Selecting a row makes **Locate**, **Soft Follow**, **Inspect**, and **Audition** available for that resident. **Discover** opens the existing seed and specimen controls.

**Locate** places a temporary mark on the resident for about five seconds. If the resident is out of frame, the app makes one cut to a stationary view. **Atlas** marks residents, active Places, notable structures, and the last perspective target. Tapping a resident selects it so it can be followed, inspected, or heard. **Focus** clears guide marks and hides panels while keeping the sound and scene running.

**Recent moments** lists a short history of observations from the simulation. Waiting can precede a normal gesture. A meeting can bring a small greeting. Resident forms settle into distinct sleeping postures when they reach a rest site. The list also reflects returns, encounters, and imitation. These moments leave the camera under the viewer's control.
