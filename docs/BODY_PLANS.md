# Phase A body plans

| Plan | Form | Motion | Reference specimens |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| HINGE | Jointed plates, negative space, pins, two grounded limbs, and an offset lever | Steps, gathers for a hop, folds, and balances after a stumble | Fold of Doubt, Left on Read, The Apology |
| VESSEL | Parametric open envelope with a faceted core and nested arcs | Drifts, contracts, tilts, and pulses. The core lags the outer form | Weather Jar, Second Skin, Air in Pieces |
| RELAY | A chain of differently sized, perforated modules with connector rods | Stretches, coils, and passes delayed motion from the leading segment to the tail | Long Errand, After You, Slow Sentence |

The `bodyPlans` registry stores the geometry and rig builder, supported actions, and nominal obstacle clearance. Genome generation sets module count, proportions, aperture, curvature, surface material, and behavioral traits from a seed. Reference identifiers reproduce their authored genomes. Six material presets supply reflectance, roughness, and translucency. Specimen meshes and their materials are disposed on removal or when an inspection model is replaced.

Phase A registers the three investigated plans. Assembly remains in the roadmap after the core art and motion directions are established.
