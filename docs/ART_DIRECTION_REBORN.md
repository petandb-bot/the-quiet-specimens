# Reborn art direction

Artificial Garden presents a constructed habitat for objects with apparently independent intentions. The ground is an ivory slab with dark green and rust layers at its edge. A perforated oxide-colored wall establishes the main vertical plane. A cobalt armature and suspended ochre mineral balance it across the deck. Empty areas between these features make the residents easier to follow.

The oblique orthographic camera sets the reading order across the diorama. A low-perspective camera shows the construction and shadows in Inspect and Action. Directional light models bevels and recesses, while an environment map makes the difference between lacquer, metallic surfaces, and thin membranes visible. The surrounding plane carries the platform's cast shadow. Color comes primarily from the surfaces and the limited palette.

The controls sit at the perimeter of the scene and can be hidden in View. The construction language draws on articulated abstract sculpture, limited palettes, and deliberate gaps in motion. Each specimen uses original procedural geometry.
